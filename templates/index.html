📑 Project Base Paper & References

"An Intelligent Research Assistance System using Hybrid Knowledge-Based Reasoning and Heuristic Search Algorithms"

Abstract

"In this paper, we propose ResearchMind AI, an Intelligent Agent designed to automate the synthesis of academic literature and the generation of structured research roadmaps. The system utilizes a dual-layer architecture: a Knowledge Representation Layer for automated summarization and domain inference, and a Symbolic AI Layer for decision support. Specifically, the system employs Forward Chaining (Unit 3) for knowledge-based reasoning to derive research recommendations from extracted facts, and Best-First Search (Unit 2) to rank generated research plans based on a Heuristic Evaluation (Unit 2) of feasibility, novelty, and resource suitability. The integration of these techniques allows for a scalable, reasoning-capable assistant that bridges the gap between raw data extraction and actionable research methodology."

📚 Foundational Syllabus Concepts (Unit 1-4)
  • Reasoning: Forward Chaining & Knowledge-Based Agents (Unit 3).
  • Search & Planning: Heuristic Functions, Best-First Search, A* (Unit 2).
  • Strategy Decomp: Hierarchical Planning & Goal Decomposition (Unit 4).
